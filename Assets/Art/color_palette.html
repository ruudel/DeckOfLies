Captain Tsubasa 2 influence: Deep saturated backgrounds with vibrant pops of color. The dark purples and blues act as the "stage" while Gold, Crimson, and Salmon provide drama — just like how CT2 uses dark stadiums with explosive character close-ups.

Twin Peaks influence: The Occult purple, the red curtain-inspired Crimson, and the eerie Ether green all nod to the show's visual language. Parchment text on dark panels evokes that unsettling, noir-ish feeling.

Recommended pixel resolution: 320×240 (NES-era) scaled up to your window size with nearest-neighbor filtering for that crisp retro look.
